# TORA & TORY — 5-Page Brand Website (React + Vite + Tailwind CSS)

A faithful, responsive React replica of [toratory.com](https://toratory.com/) expanded into a comprehensive 5-page digital house for the Indian design-led, gender-fluid demi-fine jewellery brand.

---

## 🏛️ The 5 Pages

1. **Home (`#/home` or `/`)** — [HomePage.jsx](file:///E:/Websites%20for%20client/shopify%20projects/toratory-demo/src/pages/HomePage.jsx)
   - Rotating announcement bar (*Global Shipping*, *Complimentary Returns*, *Limited Production*).
   - Hero campaign chapters for **GYRE S/S 2026**, **WRISTWEAR**, **EARRINGS**, and **RINGS**.
   - Interactive 8-look Lookbook grid with pulsing hotspot pins and multi-image popover cards.
   - Core philosophy, provocations on Karigars, and the TORA & TORY manifesto.

2. **Shop / Collections (`#/shop`)** — [ShopPage.jsx](file:///E:/Websites%20for%20client/shopify%20projects/toratory-demo/src/pages/ShopPage.jsx)
   - Category filtering: *All*, *Rings*, *Wristwear*, *Earrings*, *Necklaces*.
   - Sorting by featured curation and price in INR (Low to High / High to Low).
   - Dynamic product cards with hover-swap secondary angles, quick view, and instant bag additions.
   - Craftsmanship specs (*Proprietary Bronze*, *The Cliff Setting*, *Numbered Archive*).

3. **The GYRE Collection (`#/gyre`)** — [GyrePage.jsx](file:///E:/Websites%20for%20client/shopify%20projects/toratory-demo/src/pages/GyrePage.jsx)
   - Curatorial essay and fluid dynamic concept: oceanic vortexes frozen into wearable metal sculpture.
   - Technical breakdown of the patented **Cliff Setting** innovation (45° internal shear tension mounts).
   - Focused portfolio of Gyre pieces (*Gulf Cuff*, *Le Morne Necklace*, *Shoal Ring*, *Rossby Collar*, *Isobar Ring*, *Breaker Ring*).

4. **About The House (`#/about`)** — [AboutPage.jsx](file:///E:/Websites%20for%20client/shopify%20projects/toratory-demo/src/pages/AboutPage.jsx)
   - **Inception:** It began with frustration. Homage to millennia of Indian Karigars and unhurried heirloom metallurgy.
   - **Conceptualisation:** The Punjabi origin of *Tora* (presence, confidence, character) and *Tory*, celebrating gender-neutral self-expression without labels.
   - **Execution:** Innovation as a daily discipline, proprietary bronze alloys, and natural gemstones.

5. **Contact & Private Salon (`#/contact`)** — [ContactPage.jsx](file:///E:/Websites%20for%20client/shopify%20projects/toratory-demo/src/pages/ContactPage.jsx)
   - Studio coordinates in New Delhi, India.
   - WhatsApp private concierge line and atelier hours.
   - Interactive consultation form for private salon appointments, bespoke ring fittings, and archive piece inquiries.
   - Client care commitments: complimentary global insured transit & 14-day returns.

---

## 🛠️ Global Features
- **Pure White Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean high-fashion background.
- **Slide-Out Cart Drawer**: Live item count, quantity modifiers, complimentary shipping tracker, and checkout.
- **Quick-View Product Modal**: High-res image carousel, ring sizing options, composition details, and instant addition.
- **Live Search Modal**: Real-time filtering across titles, materials, and categories.
- **Newsletter Subscription Popup**: 25% welcome privilege modal.
